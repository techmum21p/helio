## AI Assessment
Ramon is a wealthy, first-class urban municipality, making it an attractive market for premium rooftop solar due to high household purchasing power. However, its exceptionally low solar irradiance score significantly dampens the economic potential for individual installations compared to other Philippine locations. The upcoming large-scale Limbauan solar project in nearby San Pablo creates a complex dynamic of increased regional solar awareness but potential price competition.

**Key Opportunity:** Target high-income commercial establishments and affluent homeowners with premium, space-efficient solar solutions (e.g., high-efficiency panels, aesthetic designs) and potentially paired with battery storage, leveraging the high income score and urban setting to overcome the lower solar yield.

**Key Risk:** The commissioning of the large-scale Limbauan Solar Power Project (40MW) by 2025 will likely increase electricity supply and potentially lower wholesale power costs in the local cooperative grid, which could reduce the relative economic advantage and urgency for individual rooftop solar investments in Ramon.
