## AI Assessment
Bantayan, as a 1st-class urban municipality with very high solar irradiance, represents a strong candidate for commercial and industrial solar adoption, particularly among its tourism and hospitality businesses. The high income classification and excellent solar resource (0.87 score) are compelling fundamentals. However, the moderate population score and its island geography (part of the Bantayan Island group) mean grid infrastructure and project logistics are critical considerations.

**Key Opportunity:** Target the tourism and resort sector on Bantayan Island for rooftop solar installations, leveraging its high irradiance to offer hotels and resorts significant savings on electricity costs—a major operational expense for island-based businesses.

**Key Risk:** The municipality's island location presents logistical risks for project development, equipment transport, and ongoing maintenance, potentially increasing costs and timelines compared to mainland Cebu. Furthermore, grid stability and capacity on the island could be a limiting factor for larger installations.
