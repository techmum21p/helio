**Key Risk:** The extremely low solar irradiance score (0.11) is a fundamental physical constraint; marketing must carefully manage energy yield expectations to avoid customer dissatisfaction and protect brand reputation, as the return on investment period will be significantly longer than in provinces like Palawan or Zambales.
