## AI Assessment
Kabacan presents a classic case of high economic potential but challenging solar fundamentals. Its status as a 1st-class urban municipality indicates strong commercial capacity and likely grid infrastructure for larger solar projects. However, its low solar irradiance score suggests it is not among the top locations in the country for solar generation efficiency, which could affect project ROI calculations.

**Key Opportunity:** Target commercial/industrial rooftops and local institutions (like the University of Southern Mindanao campus) for behind-the-meter solar installations, where high daytime energy consumption from businesses and facilities can offset the lower irradiance and provide direct, visible savings.

**Key Risk:** The very low solar irradiance score (0.13) is a significant technical and financial risk, potentially making standard residential or utility-scale solar projects less economically attractive compared to higher-irradiance regions in the Philippines.
