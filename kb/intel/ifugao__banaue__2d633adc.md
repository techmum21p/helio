## AI Assessment
Banaue's primary economic driver is tourism centered on its world-famous Rice Terraces, making commercial solar adoption for hotels, restaurants, and souvenir shops a logical initial market. However, its low solar irradiance score, remote mountainous location, and rural classification indicate significant infrastructure and logistical challenges that would hamper both grid-connected and off-grid solar deployment at scale.

**Key Opportunity:** Target the tourism hospitality sector (lodges, restaurants, tour operators) for rooftop solar installations, leveraging the area's high business ratings (4.56/5) and stable tourist flow to help businesses reduce volatile electricity costs.

**Key Risk:** The municipality's extreme terrain, remote location, and limited industrial base (only 1 commercial anchor found) severely increase logistics and installation costs for any large-scale solar project, making financial returns difficult to achieve.
