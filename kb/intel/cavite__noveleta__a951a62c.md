## AI Assessment
Noveleta presents a paradoxical opportunity for solar; despite its extremely low solar irradiance score, its classification as a 1st-class income urban municipality indicates high purchasing power and energy demand, making the case for solar less about peak yield and more about energy cost management and commercial sustainability initiatives. The proximity to Metro Manila and existing solar business presence suggest a market that is aware of the technology, though its coastal, cloudy climate is a fundamental constraint.

**Key Opportunity:** Target the high-income commercial and residential sector with grid-tied solar systems focused on reducing electricity bills from Meralco, leveraging the area's strong economic activity and established solar businesses (like HHOT Solar) to build partnerships or capture maintenance contracts.

**Key Risk:** The very low solar irradiance significantly reduces the energy output and financial ROI per installed kWp, making it difficult to compete with better-sited regions on a pure cost-per-kWh basis and potentially limiting widespread residential adoption.
