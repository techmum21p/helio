## AI Assessment
Lubang presents a strong technical case for solar with very high irradiance (0.90 score), making it excellent for generation. However, its rural, 4th-class income status and small population (0.30 score) severely limit commercial and residential market size, as evidenced by the web data showing an economy dominated by schools and basic health services rather than power-hungry industries. The market here is likely limited to institutional and off-grid niche applications rather than broad commercial rollout.

**Key Opportunity:** Targeted installation for public institutions like the identified schools (e.g., Lubang Integrated Elementary School) and the Lubang District Hospital, which are energy-intensive anchors in an otherwise small commercial landscape.

**Key Risk:** The extreme island logistics—Lubang Island's remoteness across the Verde Island Passage—will significantly increase transportation, installation, and maintenance costs, potentially negating the high irradiance benefit for all but the most well-funded or subsidized projects.
