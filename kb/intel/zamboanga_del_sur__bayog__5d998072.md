## AI Assessment
Bayog's unique position as a high-income 1st-class rural municipality provides significant purchasing power for solar investment, which is critical as its solar irradiance (1,329 kWh/kWp/yr) is notably below the national average. The presence of a commercial anchor and strong local business ratings suggest viable demand for commercial and industrial rooftop solar to offset energy costs. However, regional developments, like the planned Mini Hydroelectric Plant at nearby Josefina, indicate a competitive push for diversified renewable energy in Zamboanga del Sur.

**Key Opportunity:** Leverage the municipality's high income classification to target the commercial anchor and the cluster of well-rated local businesses (avg 4.59/5) with solar-as-a-service or leasing models to help them reduce operational expenses, given the limited but positive solar resource.

**Key Risk:** The very low solar irradiance score (0.09) represents a fundamental physical constraint, meaning system output will be significantly lower than in sunnier provinces, potentially making payback periods longer and sales pitches more challenging despite the strong income level.
