## AI Assessment
Polillo is a uniquely positioned rural island municipality with a strong economic base (1st class income), evidenced by local businesses and recent high-profile solar installations like the one at the Barangay Rizal Health Station. The moderate solar irradiance is balanced by a clear, documented need for reliable and sustainable power in remote barangays, creating a direct and validated market for solar solutions.

**Key Opportunity:** Target community-scale solar + battery systems for critical facilities (health stations, barangay halls, schools) and the growing small business sector, leveraging the proven model and social impact demonstrated by the One Meralco Foundation project.

**Key Risk:** Logistical complexity and higher project costs for transporting materials and technicians to the island municipality, which could deter large-scale residential commercialization without a streamlined supply chain.
