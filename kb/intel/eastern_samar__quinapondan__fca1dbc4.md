## AI Assessment
Quinapondan, as a rural 4th class municipality in the typhoon-prone Eastern Visayas, presents a modest but viable solar market driven by its need for energy resilience and cost savings, though its low income level and limited commercial activity will constrain broad adoption without external support. The moderate solar irradiance (1497 kWh/kWp/year) is technically viable, and recent regional news about Guiuan's solar project creates a tangible reference point for local government and community adoption.

**Key Opportunity:** Partnering with the local government unit (LGU) to replicate the nearby Guiuan municipal building solar project model for Quinapondan's own public facilities (e.g., municipal hall, health center, schools), leveraging potential NGO or government subsidy programs.

**Key Risk:** High vulnerability to extreme weather events like typhoons, which could damage installations, disrupt the supply chain for parts, and strain the already limited municipal budget for maintenance and insurance.
