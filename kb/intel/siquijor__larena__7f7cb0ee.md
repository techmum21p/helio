## AI Assessment
Larena presents a compelling case for commercial solar given its high solar irradiance and a core of mid-range businesses (avg. price level 2.0) that could directly benefit from the predictable energy cost savings solar provides. However, as a rural, 4th class municipality, the primary opportunity likely lies in targeting these commercial/industrial anchors rather than a diffuse residential market, which is limited by the smaller population and lower average income.

**Key Opportunity:** Target the identified commercial and industrial anchors (e.g., the 3 major establishments) with solar rooftop or ground-mount leases, as these entities have the highest motivation to offset the cited industrial electricity rates (~$0.19/kWh) and provide the necessary roof/facility space.

**Key Risk:** The low population density and 4th class income classification suggest a limited pool of residential customers with the capital for solar investments, potentially capping overall market volume and making the project pipeline heavily dependent on securing a few key commercial clients.
