## AI Assessment
Liloy presents a mixed case for solar installation: its 2nd-class income status and the news of a proposed special economic zone suggest potential for commercial adoption and economic growth. However, its notably low solar irradiance score (0.14) and rural classification indicate a challenging environment for large-scale solar efficiency and likely smaller, cost-sensitive customers. The significant nearby government-backed solar project in La Libertad signals regional interest but could also divert focus and resources.

**Key Opportunity:** Leverage the potential economic stimulus from the proposed Zamboanga del Norte Special Economic Zone to target commercial and institutional solar installations (e.g., businesses, municipal buildings) as the zone develops, positioning early as a local solar service provider.

**Key Risk:** The project's viability is hampered by very low solar irradiance, which could lead to lower energy yields and longer payback periods, making it harder to compete with conventional electricity or solar projects in sunnier nearby municipalities like the one in La Libertad.
