## AI Assessment
Burgos is an emerging but complex solar market within Ilocos Norte's renewable energy hub, showing moderate technical potential but already hosting established solar infrastructure (ELPI), indicating a competitive rather than greenfield environment. **Key Opportunity:** Leverage the provincial government's offer of underutilized public spaces and the announced Dungon-Dungon Hybrid Project to propose complementary community-focused solar solutions, such as distributed rooftop or small-scale ground-mount systems, directly addressing local benefit concerns.

**Key Risk:** Potential community opposition and political scrutiny, as evidenced by online discussions about the existing ELPI solar farm and questions on local benefit from large-scale projects, could hinder project approval and execution.
