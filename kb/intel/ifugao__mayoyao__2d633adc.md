Mayoyao presents a challenging case for a solar installation company; its very low solar irradiance score and rural nature, despite a relatively higher income score for its class, indicate a community with limited commercial demand and poor solar energy yield potential. **Key Opportunity:** Leveraging the provincial government's and NGOs' focus on sustainable tourism (e.g., related to the Rice Terraces) to pilot small-scale, off-grid or micro-grid solar solutions for essential community services like water pumping systems or tourist lodges, which aligns with the area's development priorities.
