## AI Assessment
M'Lang presents a compelling commercial opportunity due to its status as a high-income, 1st-class urban center with strong economic activity. However, its extremely low solar irradiance score (0.15) is a fundamental physical constraint that significantly undermines its potential for both commercial and residential solar adoption compared to other Philippine municipalities. **Key Opportunity:** Target commercial and industrial (C&I) customers, such as the identified malls and factories, with rooftop solar solutions to offset high grid electricity costs, leveraging the municipality's strong economic base and urban density.

**Key Risk:** The municipality's very low solar irradiance (approximately 1348 kWh/kWp/year) severely limits system output and payback potential, making it difficult to offer compelling financial returns for investors and customers compared to sunnier regions.
