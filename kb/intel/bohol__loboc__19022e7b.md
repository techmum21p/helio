## AI Assessment
Loboc presents a mixed landscape for solar adoption, where high solar irradiance is counterbalanced by a small, rural population base and a primarily agricultural economy. While Bohol's provincial push for utility-scale solar and IT-BPM growth indicates a favorable macro policy environment, Loboc's local market will likely be defined by niche commercial opportunities rather than mass residential uptake. Its identity as an eco-tourism hub (centered on the Loboc River) is the key demand driver to target.

**Key Opportunity:** Target solar installations for eco-resorts, restaurants, and attractions along the Loboc River tourism corridor, where high daytime electricity demand from air conditioning and amenities aligns perfectly with peak solar generation and can offer tangible savings to business owners.

**Key Risk:** Potential community or land-use conflicts, as Loboc's economy is still fundamentally agrarian and land may be contested between utility-scale solar developers, existing agricultural use, and the preservation of its scenic, tourism-critical landscape.
