Lal-Lo presents a unique combination of high economic capacity (1st class income classification) and demonstrated community engagement with large-scale solar, as evidenced by the operational 133 MW ACEN/CleanTech project and its corporate social responsibility partnerships. This existing solar ecosystem and infrastructure readiness significantly de-risk and validate the market for complementary distributed solar solutions, particularly for its commercial and institutional anchors.

**Key Opportunity:** Leverage the established solar awareness and grid infrastructure from the utility-scale project to target commercial and institutional clients (e.g., the 2 identified commercial/industrial anchors, municipal facilities, and schools like Bical National High School) for behind-the-meter solar and microgrid installations.
