Tubaran's high income score suggests some local purchasing power, but its very low solar irradiance score (among the lowest in the Philippines) makes utility-scale solar generation uncompetitive. The absence of major commercial/industrial anchors and the municipality's remote, rural character indicate a limited market for large commercial rooftop projects.
