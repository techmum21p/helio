## AI Assessment
Busuanga presents a compelling market for solar installations due to its excellent solar resource (score 0.90) and high income classification, which provides the financial capacity for investment. Its strategic location as the gateway to the Coron island tourism hub and the news of the impending Palawan Mega Ecozone suggest growing commercial demand for reliable, clean energy. However, its low population score reflects a dispersed, rural demographic, which will shape project types and scale.

**Key Opportunity:** Developing solar-powered microgrids or hybrid systems specifically for the high-end tourism resorts and dive shops on Busuanga and nearby Coron, leveraging its high irradiance and the tourism economy to sell reliable, premium green energy.

**Key Risk:** The municipality's remote, island geography poses significant logistical challenges and costs for equipment transport, installation crews, and ongoing maintenance, potentially eroding margins despite the high solar score.
