## AI Assessment
Sagñay, a rural coastal municipality in Camarines Sur with a strong income classification, presents a promising market for solar solutions, particularly for protecting livelihoods. Its decent solar irradiance score and the provincial push for green investments align well with the needs of its fishing-dependent economy, where reliable power is critical for cold storage. The relatively small business base, however, indicates the primary market is likely residential and small commercial users rather than large industrial anchors.

**Key Opportunity:** Target the fishing and aquaculture sector with solar-powered cold storage and ice-making facilities to reduce post-harvest losses and provide reliable power in an area susceptible to typhoons and grid outages.

**Key Risk:** The municipality's coastal location in a typhoon belt presents a direct physical risk to solar installations, requiring robust engineering and potentially higher insurance costs, which could deter adoption despite strong economic indicators.
