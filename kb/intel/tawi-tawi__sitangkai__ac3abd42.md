## AI Assessment
Sitangkai presents a high-potential but logistically challenging market. Its perfect solar irradiance score and 1st-class income classification indicate strong technical and financial demand for solar solutions. However, its remote, island-based geography in the Sulu Sea creates significant operational hurdles for installation and maintenance, which is likely reflected in the low local business price level.

**Key Opportunity:** Partner with or build upon the existing "Project SAHAYA" (Solar Home System Program) initiative by NAPOCOR, leveraging its established community trust and logistical framework to scale commercial or residential solar installations in the island barangays.

**Key Risk:** High logistical costs and complexity for equipment transport, skilled labor deployment, and after-sales service to Sitangkai's remote island communities, potentially eroding profit margins despite high demand.
