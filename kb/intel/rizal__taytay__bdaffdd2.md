## AI Assessment
Taytay is a highly urbanized, affluent 1st-class municipality adjacent to Metro Manila, making it a prime market for commercial and industrial rooftop solar due to its dense concentration of businesses, warehouses, and its status as a major economic hub in Rizal. However, its relatively lower solar irradiance (compared to other provinces) suggests that project economics will rely more on high commercial electricity rates and consistent daytime demand rather than maximum solar generation potential.

**Key Opportunity:** Target the expanding commercial and logistics parks, such as the new RLX Taytay warehouse, for large-scale rooftop solar PPA (Power Purchase Agreement) projects, capitalizing on the high energy consumption of these facilities and the municipality's top-tier income classification to support premium installations.

**Key Risk:** The municipality's below-average solar irradiance score could limit the energy output and financial returns of solar installations, potentially making projects less attractive compared to sunnier regions if not carefully offset by very high retail electricity prices.
