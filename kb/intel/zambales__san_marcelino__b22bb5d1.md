## AI Assessment
San Marcelino presents a unique, high-potential market as a rural municipality with exceptional income classification (1st class) and very high solar irradiance, already proven by its hosting of the country's largest solar farm, the 585 MW SanMar Solar project. The town's economic vitality and established renewable energy infrastructure create a strong foundation for solar adoption, though the large-scale project also dominates the local landscape and conversation.

**Key Opportunity:** Target the established local economic ecosystem and high-income households for distributed solar solutions (rooftop PV, community solar), leveraging the high solar awareness and infrastructure investment already catalyzed by the SanMar Solar project's community initiatives.

**Key Risk:** The municipality's solar market and land use are heavily dominated and potentially saturated by the massive SanMar Solar farm project, which could limit available land for new large-scale developments and create a perception that solar is "already done," stifling opportunities for smaller, distributed players.
