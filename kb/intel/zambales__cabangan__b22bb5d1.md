## AI Assessment
Cabangan, known as a quiet surf town with emerging eco-tourism along Zambales' coast, shows strong potential for commercial rooftop solar due to its high income classification and active tourism businesses, despite a relatively small population base. The confirmed 56.77-MW utility-scale solar project in the municipality indicates significant solar resource validation and existing investor interest, creating a favorable environment for complementary distributed generation projects.

**Key Opportunity:** Target the tourism and hospitality sector—specifically beachfront resorts and hotels—for commercial rooftop solar installations to reduce their high operational energy costs and capitalize on their demonstrated willingness to invest.

**Key Risk:** The large, already-advanced 56.77-MW Cabangan solar project could saturate the local utility grid's absorption capacity or lead to land-use/permitting sensitivities that complicate new distributed solar projects.
