## AI Assessment
Makati City's prime positioning as the Philippines' financial capital, with its extremely high commercial density and corporate wealth (Income Score: 1.00), creates a strong demand for premium, sustainable energy solutions despite its relatively lower solar irradiance (0.31). The presence of existing solar projects within its business district and its formal commitment to SDG 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy) indicate a market receptive to rooftop and building-integrated solar installations, particularly targeting the numerous high-rise commercial and office buildings that define its skyline.

**Key Opportunity:** Targeting retrofitting projects for Makati's vast inventory of existing Grade-A office towers and malls for rooftop or facade solar PV systems, leveraging corporate ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) mandates and the city's documented sustainability goals.

**Key Risk:** High land and real estate costs severely limit the feasibility of ground-mounted solar farms; opportunities are almost exclusively confined to rooftop and vertical installations on existing structures, making project viability heavily dependent on individual building owner agreements and structural assessments.
