# Solar Installation Opportunity Report: Laguna

**Date:** May 28, 2026
**Location Analyzed:** Laguna, Region IV-A (CALABARZON), Philippines

## Executive Summary
Laguna presents a diverse and promising market for solar installation, with 10 identified municipalities offering varying strengths. The total addressable market is characterized by strong solar irradiance across the region, particularly in municipalities like Kalayaan (1731 kWh/kWp/yr) and Majayjay (1692 kWh/kWp/yr). The top opportunities are concentrated in areas with robust commercial activity and higher income classifications, such as Bay and the City of Cabuyao. Key to market penetration will be developing tailored financing solutions for price-sensitive segments and targeting the tourism, commercial, and industrial sectors that show the clearest ROI.

## Detailed Profiles

1.  **Liliw:** Liliw's opportunity is anchored in its renowned footwear industry and active commerce. With a 3rd-class income level, the market consists of price-sensitive SMEs and households. The strategy should focus on demonstrating clear ROI to workshops and cafes to reduce operational costs and improve energy security.

2.  **Kalayaan:** This municipality boasts near-maximal solar potential and an urban setting. The key opportunity lies in leveraging community awareness from the adjacent Paete Wind Farm to market solar as a complementary investment for new housing developments and small businesses. The main risk is the market's price sensitivity.

3.  **Bay:** As a 1st-class municipality, Bay has strong purchasing power. Its numerous hot spring resorts and spas represent a prime commercial target due to their high energy consumption for heating. Solar can directly reduce their operational costs and enhance their sustainability branding.

4.  **City of San Pablo:** This area is rated HIGH tier due to its solid solar resources. While its rural setting limits mass-market volume, the presence of tourism and commercial anchors like resorts and cafes creates a viable segment for targeted sales approaches.

5.  **Luisiana:** This semi-rural, 3rd-class municipality shows community-level acceptance of solar (via streetlights). The opportunity is in building on this with financing programs for cost-conscious households and small agri-businesses. However, very long payback periods are a significant risk due to low income levels.

## Recommended Action Plan

1.  **Develop Tiered Financing Packages:** Create and promote accessible financing or lease-to-own options specifically for the residential and SME segments in price-sensitive, lower-income class municipalities (e.g., Luisiana, Calauan, Los Baños).
2.  **Launch Targeted B2B Campaigns:** Focus immediate sales efforts on the commercial sectors identified in higher-opportunity areas: tourism (hot springs in Bay), light manufacturing (footwear in Liliw), and institutional clients (UPLB, factories in Cabuyao).
3.  **Pilot a Developer Partnership:** Approach real estate developers in rapidly urbanizing cities like Santa Rosa and Calauan to integrate solar installations as a standard or premium feature in new housing projects, aligning with "green city" initiatives.

## Methodology Note
This report synthesizes multiple data points to create a composite final score (0-1) for each municipality. The analysis integrates a **geo-score** based on solar irradiance, **economic and demographic data** including income classification, population density, and urban status, and **market activity indicators** from commercial listings and existing solar projects. All solar generation estimates (kWh/kWp/year) are derived from geographical models.
