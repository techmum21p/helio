## Detailed Profiles
**1. Cavite - Municipality 4 (HIGH Tier)**  
This municipality is the standout market with a perfect solar irradiance score (1.00) and a strong income level (0.75). The opportunity lies in the significant demand from its industrial zones and growing commercial sector, which are likely early adopters seeking to manage operational costs. The primary risk is market saturation and price competition, as an established local installer (Ecoplus Solar Inc.) is already active.

**2. Cavite - Municipality 8 (MEDIUM Tier)**  
This area offers strong potential driven by top-tier income levels and significant commercial growth, with new developments like Vermosa and Evo City. The opportunity is to target affluent homeowners and new commercial constructions with integrated solar solutions. The risk involves high competition from established local installers and potentially selective customers, which could pressure margins.

**3. Cavite - Municipality 6 (MEDIUM Tier)**  
While featuring perfect solar irradiance (1.00), this municipality's extremely low population density limits residential demand. The major opportunity is within its significant agricultural sector (91% of land use), offering solar installations to power irrigation, processing, and storage. The key risk is that a purely home-focused strategy is unviable due to the sparse population.

**4. Cavite - Municipality 5 (MEDIUM Tier)**  
This municipality combines high solar irradiance and population density with evident regional commercial growth. The opportunity is to target the commercial and industrial sector near growing hubs like Landers and Ayala Malls Vermosa. The severe risk is the critically low residential income level (0.00), which creates a major affordability barrier for homeowners.

**5. Cavite - Municipality 10 (MEDIUM Tier)**  
Excellent solar irradiance (0.92) and strong population density (0.73) form a viable market base, but the critically low income level (0.00) hampers residential adoption. The opportunity aligns with Cavite's status as a "next commercial destination," targeting B2B installations on new warehouses, offices, and retail spaces. The risk is that the low-income residential market remains largely inaccessible without financing solutions.

## Methodology Note
This report synthesizes a composite geospatial market score (`geo_score`) for Cavite municipalities, which combines normalized data on solar irradiance, population density, and income levels. These scores are then factored into a `final_score` alongside estimated solar yield (`solar_kwh_estimate`) to classify municipalities into HIGH, MEDIUM, and LOW opportunity tiers.
