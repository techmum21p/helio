# Solar Installation Opportunity Report: Liliw, Laguna

## Executive Summary
The analysis identifies **Liliw, Laguna** as a medium-tier but viable target for solar installation services. While its geographic solar potential is moderate, the municipality's significant population, urban status, and tourism-driven commercial activity present a focused opportunity. The key recommendation is to develop a targeted sales strategy for its numerous local restaurants and commercial establishments, which stand to benefit most from reduced operational costs and enhanced eco-appeal.

## Detailed Profiles

### Liliw
**Liliw presents a compelling case for solar adoption due to its significant population density and emerging commercial activity.** The town is classified as urban with a population of 122,797 and hosts a bustling business scene, including popular restaurants like Palaisdaan and Sulyap Gallery, alongside recent local solar installations. This creates a solid foundation for targeted sales. The primary **opportunity** lies in partnering with these numerous hospitality and commercial entities to help lower their electricity bills and market themselves as sustainable. However, the main **risk** stems from Liliw's relatively lower solar irradiance score of 0.35. This could lead to higher system costs per kWh generated, potentially slowing the payback period and making cost-conscious customers in this third-class income municipality more hesitant.

## Recommended Action Plan

1.  **Develop a Commercial & Tourism-Focused Pitch:** Create marketing materials and case studies that speak directly to the pain points of Liliw's restaurants and shops, emphasizing ROI through energy cost savings and the marketing benefit of "green" branding for attracting tourists.
2.  **Initiate Consultative Outreach:** Schedule meetings with managers/owners of the identified commercial establishments (e.g., Palaisdaan, Sulyap Gallery) to conduct preliminary site assessments and provide customized quotes that address the specific payback period concerns inherent in the area's solar profile.
3.  **Leverage Local Proof Points:** Engage with existing local solar installations mentioned in the assessment. If possible, secure testimonials or create anonymized case studies from these early adopters to build trust and demonstrate tangible results within the same municipal context.
