# Solar Installation Opportunity Report: Laguna

## Executive Summary

The solar installation market in Laguna presents a balanced mix of opportunities across 20 municipalities, characterized by strong solar irradiance but varied economic capacities. The **City of Santa Rosa** emerges as the top-ranked target due to its high-tier status and industrial/commercial base, offering the most immediate revenue potential. The primary opportunity lies in **targeting the commercial and industrial segments** within Laguna's urban and economic hubs, though success will require tailored financing solutions for residential and small business markets in lower-income areas.

## Detailed Profiles (Top 5 Targets)

**1. Liliw**
Liliw's economy, driven by its slipper industry and growing tourism, offers a viable commercial solar market, particularly for tourism-facing businesses. Its 3rd-class income classification, however, may limit widespread residential adoption and constrain the purchasing power of smaller local businesses, making targeted marketing and financing essential.

**2. Kalayaan**
Kalayaan boasts exceptionally high solar irradiance (score 0.96), making it technically ideal for solar generation. The opportunity is primarily in the residential and small commercial segments to offset electricity costs for air conditioning. The key risk is the municipality's lower income classification, which makes customer financing and flexible payment plans critical for overcoming high upfront costs.

**3. City of San Pablo**
San Pablo City is a moderately attractive market with a higher income classification (2nd class) but a relatively small, rural population. The opportunity targets the agricultural and small commercial sectors, such as lakeside resorts and food processors. The main risk is existing competition from established local solar providers, which could lead to intense price pressure.

**4. Bay**
As a 1st-class municipality and host of the provincial capitol, Bay's market is skewed toward residential adoption driven by higher disposable incomes. The primary opportunity is in targeting residential subdivisions and new housing developments. A potential risk is the local economy's heavy ties to government and service sectors, which could dampen household spending on large-capex items like solar during economic slowdowns.

**5. Luisiana**
Luisiana, known for its agricultural base and "pandan" industry, presents a targeted commercial solar opportunity in the agricultural and food processing sector. These energy-intensive yet cost-sensitive businesses can benefit from reduced operational costs. The significant risk is the municipality's 3rd-class income status, which may limit the capital expenditure capacity of local businesses and homeowners.

## Recommended Action Plan

1.  **Prioritize Industrial Outreach in Santa Rosa:** The sales team should immediately begin outreach to the automotive factories and manufacturing plants concentrated along Laguna Boulevard. The focus should be on large-scale rooftop and carpark solar solutions that offer significant operational savings and hedge against high electricity rates.

2.  **Develop a Tourism & Agriculture Financing Package:** For the medium-tier municipalities like Liliw, Luisiana, and San Pablo, create a dedicated financing product (e.g., leasing, partner loans) targeting tourism establishments and agri-businesses. This addresses the key risk of upfront cost barriers in these areas.

3.  **Pilot an Institutional Project in Los Baños:** Initiate a formal proposal to the University of the Philippines Los Baños (UPLB) for a pilot agri-solar or campus solar project. Success here can serve as a powerful reference case for the academic and research community across the region.

## Methodology Note

This report synthesizes data from solar irradiance databases, municipal income classifications from the Department of Finance, and population and urbanization statistics from the Philippine Statistics Authority. The final score is a composite metric weighing geographic solar potential, economic indicators, and market activity signals.
